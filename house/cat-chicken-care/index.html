Daily Routine

Cat & Chicken Care

Tick each task as you finish it. The list resets every morning. Read the notes on each item — they stop the small mistakes (who eats what, closing the broom, not feeding at night).

Morning

start of day
When you go in or out the door, use the broom to keep Mimim inside. He is not neutered, so he will try to slip out.

Noon

midday
The morning batch of boiled fish covers breakfast and lunch. You do not boil a second batch for lunch.

Afternoon

before it gets dark

Night

end of day
Every time the door opens at night, use the broom against the door so Mimim does not get out.
